License format:
    XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX (issued by Gumroad)
    Your license key must be entered together with the email address used

Data handling:
    When using Thought Tidy Hosted AI:
    - Your text, email address, and license key are transmitted over HTTPS

      It is not stored by us after the response is returned.
      See OpenRouter's privacy policy: https://openrouter.ai/privacy
    - We track call counts and token counts per email address for billing
      and limit enforcement. We do not log the content of your requests.

    Subscriptions are billed monthly through Gumroad. Cancelling your
